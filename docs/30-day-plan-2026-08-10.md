## 1. The data this plan is built on

Two GSC exports and one GA4 read, both already analysed in this repo:

| Source | Window | Impressions | Clicks | CTR | Avg position |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `docs/gsc-ctr-improvement-2026-08-04.md` | 28 days to 4 Aug | 9,567 | 54 | 0.56% | 17.35 |
| Issue #57 (page-level, authoritative) | 4 Jul – 5 Aug | 11,061 | 56 | **0.51%** | 17.7 |

Alongside that, from GA4: **key events = 0**, **total revenue = ₹0**.

The programme measures itself against the **4 Jul – 5 Aug page-level** figures, because issue #57
established that the page-level export is the authoritative one — the query-level export drops 67%
of impressions and 86% of clicks to anonymised-query filtering, so site-wide CTR must never be
computed from query-level rows. Query-level data is used only for intent vocabulary.

### The four findings that shape every day below

1. **This is a click-through problem, not a ranking problem.** 6,412 impressions — 58% of all —
   sit at positions 3–10 and produced 48 clicks. That is a **0.75% CTR** where 5–10% is normal for
   the band, a gap worth roughly **300–600 clicks per month**.
2. **We cannot see what works.** Zero GA4 key events means no calculator can be shown to produce
   value, and 14 pages report `0.00s` engagement time while still firing events.
3. **A third of impressions land on pages nobody visits.** 74 pages drew impressions and zero
   visitors (3,727 impressions). 25 of the then-53 tool slugs drew zero views — and the Aug 3–9
   sprint then added ~28 more tools, taking the site to 59.
4. **Freshness is the only format that converts.** `/financial-updates/*` pages hit 1.7–2.7% CTR
   while evergreen calculators at the *same positions* convert at 0.0–0.5%. The calculator SERPs
   are being satisfied in place by AI Overviews and Google's inline widgets.

### What this plan deliberately does not do

It does not add calculators. Issue #57 drew that line at 25-of-53 slugs with zero traffic; the site
now has 59 tools and less traffic per tool than when the line was drawn. Day 23 (#78) triages the
portfolio instead, and Day 37 (#92) sets the evidence bar a future tool must clear.

Phase 4 assumes clicks are arriving — an assumption that may turn out to be wrong.

## 4. Targets

Carried forward from `docs/gsc-ctr-improvement-2026-08-04.md` §11 and measured on Day 36 (#91):

- **≥ 2% CTR** on the page-one opportunity set (ITR-2 guide, gratuity guide, broker comparison,
  capital-gains calculator, SIP calculator, 8th CPC calculator).
- **Non-zero clicks** on `capital-gains-tax-calculator-india` and `sip-calculator-india`, which sit
  at 0 clicks from positions 4.3 and 3.3.
- **Position 3–10 band CTR** materially above the 0.75% baseline, reported in absolute clicks.
- **GA4 key events > 0**, which requires the manual admin steps from Day 8 (#63).
- **Zero-visitor page count** below the baseline of 74.

These are goals, not guarantees. Search CTR depends on SERP features, competitors and query mix,
and no day in this programme is a controlled experiment — Day 36 is required to state its confounds.

## 5. Standing constraints

Every issue in this programme inherits these:

- **No new calculators** without evidence clearing the Day 37 bar.
- **No regulatory figure changes without a cited primary source** and an on-page verification date.
  Tax, gratuity, PPF, SSY and interest-rate content in this repo has already required correction
  (#37, #38, and the broker-charges error fixed on 4 Aug).
- **Titles ≤ 60 characters, never leading with "Free"; descriptions 140–160 characters.**
- **No slug changes without a one-hop 301** and removal from `app/sitemap.ts`.
- **No page may cannibalise another** — distinct `targetKeyword` per page, linter-enforced.
- **Keep the existing component vocabulary**: `QuickAnswerBox`, `FactsTable`, `AnswerEngineSummary`,
  `DiscoverHeroImage`, `CalculatorPresets`.
- **All analytics through the typed helper** in `lib/analytics.ts`, guarded for `window.gtag`
  being undefined. No user-entered financial value is ever sent to analytics.
- **`npm run validate`, `npm run lint`, `npm run test`, `npm run build` must pass** on every PR.
