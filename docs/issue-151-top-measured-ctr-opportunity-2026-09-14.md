# Issue #151 — top measured CTR opportunity

Date: 14 September 2026

## Selected URL

`/tools/8th-pay-commission-salary-calculator-india`

The fresh RupeeKit 90-day analytics export ending 12 September 2026 records:

- 3,356 organic Google Search impressions
- 17 organic Google Search clicks
- 0.51% CTR
- 6.89 average position

This is the highest-impression specific calculator/tool URL in the fresh export that is already averaging positions 3–10 and still has weak CTR. The broader `/8th-pay-commission` status hub has materially more impressions, but the current programme explicitly says to review its post-8 September baseline before another title edit. This experiment therefore focuses on the calculator and separates calculation intent from status/news intent.

The query export is sitewide rather than page-query joined, so query rows are used only as cluster context, not falsely attributed to this URL. The 8th-pay-commission cluster is dominated by status, date, news and fitment-factor queries; the calculator page should therefore lead with calculation/scenario intent while the hub owns verified status updates.

## CTR experiment

Before title: `8th Pay Commission Status, Date & Salary Calculator`

After title: `8th Pay Commission Salary Calculator | Fitment Scenarios`

The meta description now leads with salary-scenario comparison, fitment factor, HRA and deductions, while preserving the boundary that there is no published final fitment factor or implementation order. The H1 and answer-first opening now describe the page as an unofficial salary-scenario calculator instead of competing with the status hub.

### Target

Minimum test target: **0.75% CTR** over the next settled 28-day comparison window, while monitoring impressions and average-position mix so a CTR change is not misread as a ranking-mix change.

This is a measurement target, not a ranking or traffic guarantee. A 1.0% CTR can be treated as a stretch sensitivity only, not a forecast.

## Source verification

Official status language was rechecked on 14 September 2026 against:

- 8th Central Pay Commission official site: https://8cpc.gov.in/
- About Us: https://8cpc.gov.in/about-us/
- What's New / current consultation notices: https://8cpc.gov.in/whats-new/

The official site currently shows ongoing stakeholder consultations/visits and does not publish a final fitment factor, final pay matrix or implementation order in its current notices. The calculator therefore keeps every projected salary value explicitly scenario-based.

## Guardrails

- No calculator formula, input, output, slug or canonical changed.
- No official fitment factor, revised pay or arrears entitlement is implied.
- No fake schema, reviews, ratings or guarantees were added.
- No sensitive-data collection was added.
- Existing calculation-governance tests remain authoritative.

## Measurement follow-up

After deployment, compare this exact URL over a settled 28-day window against the pre-change metrics above. Segment by device/query cohort where possible. Do not credit the title change if CTR improvement is explained primarily by a materially different position or query mix.
